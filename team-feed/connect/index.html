How it works

  1. Sign in to GitHub in this browser. Your GitHub account is your identity here; we keep no other ID.
  2. Fill in the form and press Encrypt and open GitHub. A new issue opens with the encrypted text filled in.
  3. Press Submit new issue on GitHub. Within a few minutes the platform replies on the issue with the result and a rule ID, then closes it.
