How to use

  1. Drop the photo in the box or click to choose it.
  2. Pick or type the upload limit (for example 200 KB).
  3. Click "Download JPEG" and upload the file.

FAQ

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser; the photo is never sent over the internet. For HEIC files only the converter code is downloaded — your photo stays on your device.

Why does the resolution sometimes drop?

The tool lowers quality first. If quality reaches a sensible floor and the file is still too big, it reduces resolution instead of making the image blocky.

Is location data kept in the photo?

No. The downloaded JPEG is redrawn, so GPS location and camera details (EXIF) are not in the file.

What if the site counts 1 KB as 1024 bytes?

The tool counts 1 KB as 1000 bytes, so the file is under the limit either way.
